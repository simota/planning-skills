# Traps — options

- **Straw-man alternatives**: options generated to justify a foregone conclusion are worse than no comparison, because they launder the bias. If option 2 and 3 were never candidates, admit there was one option.
- **Scoring after seeing the options**: weights chosen late get chosen to win. Fix them in `CRITERIA`.
- **Comparing on features instead of failure modes**: approaches rarely lose on capability, they lose on how they degrade. Compare the failures.
- **Omitting do-nothing**: a large share of proposed work loses to doing nothing, and only an explicit row surfaces it.
- **Recording only the winner**: the rejection reasons are what stop the same option being re-proposed next quarter. Keep the losers in the document.
