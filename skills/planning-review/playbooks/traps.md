# Traps — review

- **Reviewing for completeness instead of soundness**: a plan can fill every section and still be wrong. The nine checks are the floor; the attack pass is the review.
- **Blocking-list inflation**: every finding marked blocking trains the team to override the gate. Block on what actually breaks the plan.
- **Accepting `[verified]` at face value**: a verification tag with no pointer is an assumption with better handwriting. Spot-check the pointers.
- **Missing the reviewer bottleneck**: plans routinely account for author effort and ignore approval latency. Check who has to say yes.
- **Self-review without adjustment**: reviewing your own plan reproduces your own blind spots. Say so, and lean hardest on the silence audit.
