# Traps — estimation

- **Effort quoted as a date**: "three days of work" becomes "Thursday" only after review latency, deploy windows, and the productive fraction are applied. Keep the two columns separate all the way to the end.
- **Averaging a top-down/bottom-up disagreement**: the gap is missing work, and averaging conceals it. Find the missing tasks.
- **Buffer hidden in task estimates**: it disappears into the tasks and cannot be reported as remaining. Keep it as one visible line.
- **Estimating around an open question**: an unresolved `Q-n` on the critical path makes the number fiction. Estimate the spike instead.
- **Anchoring on the desired date**: once a date is spoken, every subsequent estimate bends toward it. Size the work before the date enters the conversation, and if it has already entered, say so and widen.
- **Optimistic case treated as achievable**: the optimistic figure assumes nothing goes wrong, which is the rarest outcome. Never quote it alone.
